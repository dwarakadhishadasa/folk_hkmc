updated: 2026-11-01T10:17

- (direction) Sessions must exactly conform to the sessions.figcraft design on desktop and mobile.
- (direction) New session opens the existing session creation flow: name, location, duration, start.
- (direction) A session shows Completed in the overview once its duration has elapsed.
- (capability) CAP-1 Sessions overview lists every in-scope session with name, date, location, attendee total and automatic status, filterable by location.
- (capability) CAP-2 Status is derived, never stored: Upcoming before the window, Live now inside it, Completed after it, refreshed at the next boundary.
- (capability) CAP-3 Session detail lists attendees with name, phone and location; opening one shows the full contact record and attendance history.
- (capability) CAP-4 Reports show attendance by session, weekly check-ins, top sessions and sessions with no check-ins over a date range.
- (capability) CAP-5 The New session action opens the existing creation flow and the created session appears as Live now immediately.
- (capability) CAP-6 Mobile screens use the Campaign chevron title and pill sub-navigation; desktop uses the underline tab strip.
- (constraint) Design authority is the sessions.figcraft frame set; reuse the existing header, tab strip, stat cards, table and Campaign tokens.
- (constraint) No schema change: sessions, attendance, contacts and locations only; no status, capacity or invite column.
- (constraint) Attendee total is count of attendance rows for the session; the detail record is joined to contacts through contact_id.
- (constraint) Session creation keeps today's behaviour: attendance opens now, closes after the duration, public link enabled.
- (constraint) Scope reuses the staff gate: Admin sees the programme, Preacher their own sessions, Assistant their assigned preacher's sessions.
- (constraint) Reports are computed on read; no aggregates table, cron or manual status write.
- (constraint) Mobile session detail and contact are the designed surfaces; the saved design has no desktop session detail frame.
- (decision) Out of scope: scheduling future sessions, editing or deleting sessions, capacity or invitations, attendance correction, per-session export, call or Campaign integration.
- (assumption) Attendance is walk-in check-ins, so the overview shows a total rather than an "of N" capacity fraction.
- (assumption) Mobile contact is read-only; Call, Message and Open contact were removed from the saved design.
- (assumption) Needs attention means sessions with zero check-ins until a capacity field exists.
- (question) The saved design no longer contains a desktop Session detail frame. Restore it, or is desktop limited to Overview and Reports?
- (question) The design shows "42 of 60" and under-filled sessions but there is no capacity or invite data. Add an expected-attendees field, or show totals only?
- (question) Should New session open the existing form as a dialog from the overview, or keep the current inline form on /sessions?
- (note) Inputs: sessions.figcraft (9 frames), sessions-design-set-summary.md, current /sessions manager and /api/sessions, sessions/attendance/contacts schema.

- (direction) Absent is removed; attendance history lists attended sessions only.
- (direction) Needs attention is removed from the reports.
