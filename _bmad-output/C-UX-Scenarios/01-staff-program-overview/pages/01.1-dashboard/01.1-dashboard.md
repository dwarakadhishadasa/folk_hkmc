## Overview

**Page Purpose:** One screen that answers "how is the programme doing, and what needs me now" across contacts, sessions, attendance, preachers and campaigns — without opening five other pages.

**User Situation:** A coordinator signs in on a session day to see whether attendance is live and how it is tracking, or opens the page between sessions to check volume, attendance and preacher activity before deciding what to work on.

**Success Criteria:**
- The reader can state contacts, sessions, attendees and total attendance without leaving the page.
- A live session is identifiable in one glance, with a count that agrees with `/sessions`.
- An Admin can reach their own rows in one tap, and every section follows.
- No number on this page disagrees with the page that owns it.

**Entry Points:**
- The `Dashboard` header item (desktop pill nav and mobile bottom nav).
- Post-sign-in landing for Admin, Preacher and Assistant.
- A shared or bookmarked `/overview` or `/overview?mode=preacher` link.

**Exit Points:**
- `/sessions` — from the live band and the recent-session card.
- `/campaigns` — from the Campaign card.
- `/contact` — from the empty state and the nav.
- `/manage` — from the nav.

## Scope Model

One scope, resolved once, used by every section. The page must not define a second meaning of "own".

| Reader | `?mode=` | Rows shown |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Admin | absent, `admin`, or anything not `preacher` | Whole programme |
| Admin | `preacher` | Contacts assigned to the Admin; sessions they preach or created; attendance on those sessions; their own locations |
| Preacher | ignored | Same as Admin `preacher`, for the signed-in Preacher |
| Assistant | ignored | Same as `preacher`, resolved for `assignedPreacherUserId` — the preacher they assist |

**Predicates** (identical to `loadManagePortalData` in `lib/supabase/manage.ts`; extract one helper rather than copy a third time):

- Contacts: `assigned_preacher_id = <scope user id>`
- Sessions: `preacher_id = <scope user id>` **or** `created_by = <scope user id>`
- Attendance: rows whose `session_id` is in the scoped session set
- Locations: `users.location_ids` of the scope user

**Assistant resolution** mirrors `apps/folk/app/sessions/page.tsx`: read `assignedPreacherUserId`; the referenced staff row must be an Active Preacher, and their `location_ids` become the scope. A missing id, a missing row, a non-Preacher or an inactive Preacher is an **empty scope**, not a refusal.

**Fail-open coercion:** `resolveManageMode` turns anything that is not literally `preacher` into `admin`. Reuse it. It is safe because RLS is the enforcement point and the app-layer narrowing can only shrink the set.

**Do not** honour `mode=admin` for a Preacher or Assistant in the UI: they have no switch, and their scope is fixed. RLS already narrows them, but the page must not depend on that for correctness.

## Statistics Contract

Every figure is derived from scoped rows, never recomputed from a neighbouring figure.

### KPI band

| Object | Label | Value | Caption | Own-scope behaviour |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|---------------------|
| `overview-kpi-contacts` | Contacts | Count of contacts in scope | `In this programme` / `Assigned to you` | Narrows |
| `overview-kpi-sessions` | Sessions | Count of sessions in scope | `{n} in the last 60 days` | Narrows |
| `overview-kpi-attendees` | Attendees | Distinct `contact_id` in scoped attendance | `People who attended at least once` | Narrows |
| `overview-kpi-attendance` | Attendance | Count of scoped attendance rows | `Check-ins across all sessions` | Narrows |
| `overview-kpi-campaigns` | Active campaigns | Campaigns with status `ACTIVE` | `In this programme` / `With your contacts` | Narrows to campaigns whose scoped summary has `assigned > 0` |

`Active campaigns` and the whole Campaign section are absent when `modules.dialer` is false; the band is then four tiles.

**Why Attendees and Attendance are both present:** they answer different questions. Attendees is reach ("how many people have ever come"), Attendance is volume ("how many check-ins"). They are labelled and captioned so neither can be mistaken for the other, and neither is a ratio.

**Attendees is not intersected with the scoped contact set.** A contact assigned to another preacher who attended a session in this scope still counts — they came to this preacher's session, which is the fact the tile is reporting. Intersecting would make the tile disagree with the session's own attendee count.

### Live band

A session is live when `public_attendance_enabled` is true and `now` is at or after `attendance_opens_at` (when present) and strictly before `attendance_closes_at`. Reuse the existing predicate — it currently exists in three copies (`apps/*/app/dashboard/page.tsx`, `components/sessions-manager.tsx`); extract it to one client-safe module rather than adding a fourth.

- Count = attendance rows for that session, refreshed every 20 s while the tab is visible.
- Order: newest `attendance_opens_at` first. Cap at three rows; the rest collapse to `+N more` linking to `/sessions`.
- One `now` for the whole render, so the band and the KPI band cannot disagree about which sessions count.

### Trends

| Object | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| `overview-trend-contacts` | New contacts per quarter, grouped by `contacts.initial_contact` (fallback: `created_at`). Same grouping as `/manage`. Quarters ascending. |
| `overview-trend-attendance` | Attendees per session for the 10 most recent sessions in scope, ordered by `session_date` (fallback: `created_at`), oldest to newest. Sessions with zero attendance appear as `0`. |

### Breakdown

| Mode | Rows | Columns |
|------|------|---------|
| Admin, `mode=admin` | Every active Preacher, plus any staff id referenced by a scoped session | Preacher · Contacts · Sessions · Attendees · Last session |
| `mode=preacher` | Every location in scope | Location · Sessions · Attendees · Last session |

- Sort: attendees descending, then sessions descending, then name ascending.
- `Last session` is the most recent session date in that row, formatted `12 Oct 2026`; `—` when none.
- A row with no activity stays listed with zeros — the table's job includes showing who has no activity.

### Campaign

The five tiles and their definitions come from `CampaignOverviewStats`; this page does not restate them. Values are summed across the campaigns the page is showing, exactly as `/campaigns` does.

**Critical invariant:** the Campaign section must narrow with the same `mode` as the rest of the page. `campaignScope(staff)` returns `program` for an Admin, so `mode=preacher` needs an explicit preacher-scope override. A page where every tile narrows except the campaign funnel is worse than no switch at all.

### Recent sessions

The five most recent sessions in scope: Session · Date · Preacher · Location · Attendees.

### Explicitly excluded

**Invites.** `invite_log` is write-only today (`lib/invite-log.ts`) with no read surface, so there is no honest invite statistic to show. Adding one would be a new data path, not a dashboard layout change.

## Accessibility

- **Landmarks:** one `<main>`; each section is a `<section>` with an `aria-label` or a heading.
- **Heading order:** `h1` Dashboard → `h2` per card. The live band has no heading of its own; its `<section aria-label="Live sessions">` names it.
- **Scope switch:** wrapped in `<nav aria-label="Data scope">`; the active link carries `aria-current="page"`. The desktop and phone instances are the same links — only one is in the accessibility tree at a time.
- **Stat tiles:** a `<dl>`; each label is a `<dt>` and each value/caption pair a `<dd>`. A screen reader reads "Contacts, 272, in this programme", not "272".
- **Charts:** every chart card carries an `sr-only` sentence enumerating its data, because a Recharts SVG has no text equivalent.
- **Tables:** `<th scope="col">` on every head cell. Numbers are right of their label in reading order, not just visually.
- **Live region:** the live count is **not** an `aria-live` region. A number that changes every 20 s would interrupt the reader continuously; the `Updated {time}` line is the polite signal.
- **Motion:** the pulse is decorative and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Focus:** `DS-FocusRing` on every link and button. `--ring` is the only focus token that stays visible on both white cards and the brand header in FOLK; `--program-accent` resolves to a near-white there and must not be used for focus.
- **Colour:** every value has a text label; no figure is encoded by colour alone. The campaign funnel's amber edge is decoration, as it is on `/campaigns`.

## Technical Notes

### Data loading

- New server module: `lib/supabase/overview.ts` exporting `loadOverviewData({ staff, mode })`.
- Read only the columns the page uses:
  - contacts: `id, assigned_preacher_id, collected_by_id, initial_contact, created_at`
  - sessions: `id, name, session_date, preacher_id, location_id, created_by, public_attendance_enabled, attendance_opens_at, attendance_closes_at`
  - attendance: `id, contact_id, session_id, created_at`
  - locations: `id, name, status`
  - users: `id, name, role, status, location_ids` for referenced ids
- Use `collectPagedRows` for every read, as `loadManagePortalData` does; do not add an unbounded `.select()`.
- Do **not** reuse `loadManagePortalData`: it also loads rollups, photo paths, books and chart series the dashboard does not need, and its cost would be paid on every dashboard visit.
- One `now` per render, used for live detection, the 60-day caption and the trend window.

### Scope

- Extract the three narrowing predicates from `lib/supabase/manage.ts` into one exported helper and call it from both loaders. A second definition is how "Own scope" starts meaning two things.
- Compose role and mode as `staff.role === "Admin" ? resolveManageMode(param) : "preacher"`. Never let a query parameter widen a Preacher or Assistant.
- Assistant scope resolves through `assignedPreacherUserId`, matching `apps/folk/app/sessions/page.tsx`.

### Campaign narrowing

- `listCampaignsWithProgress(staff)` calls `campaignScope(staff)`, which returns `program` for an Admin. For `mode=preacher` the overview needs an explicit preacher-scope override — extend `listCampaignsWithProgress` with an optional scope, or add a sibling that takes one.
- Catch `campaign_scope_missing` for an Assistant with no assignment and render the section's empty state; do not fail the page.

### Live predicate

`isActiveSession` currently exists in `apps/folk/app/dashboard/page.tsx`, `apps/gita-life/app/dashboard/page.tsx` and `components/sessions-manager.tsx`. Extract one client-safe predicate (e.g. `lib/sessions/live.ts`) and use it at all four call sites — the three existing ones plus the new live band. Do not add a fourth copy.

### Verification

- `pnpm test:static` — guardrails, typecheck, lint.
- `pnpm test:unit` — scope composition, KPI derivations, quarter grouping, trend window, empty-scope resolution.
- `pnpm test:e2e "overview|smoke"` — the page's own spec plus the landing-path rows. `components/header.tsx`, `lib/auth-context.tsx`, `proxy.ts` and `lib/supabase/*` are all shared-file changes, so a full `pnpm test:e2e` is required before a PR.
- Add an `Overview` section to the feature-to-test registry in `docs/architecture/testing.md`.
- Add `/overview` to the staff-pages list in `docs/index.md`.
- Do not touch `/dashboard`. It stays reachable, out of the nav, and a retirement candidate.

### Performance

- The page is `force-dynamic` like every other staff surface; the data is operational and must not be cached across staff members.
- The route is a server component that resolves scope and loads the payload. The two charts (Recharts) and the live band are client components, so the payload must be serialisable and passed down the way `ManagePortal` receives it — no Supabase client in the browser.
- Four paged reads, one staff-name read and one campaign batch. If contact volume makes the full read too slow, replace the contact read with a count plus a grouped per-preacher query — but the KPI and the breakdown must still come from one loader so they cannot disagree.
