- (decision) Admin's Assistant inherits the Admin's personal patch (Model B), not programme scope. Patch = contacts assigned_preacher_id = owner; sessions preacher_id or created_by = owner; locations owner.location_ids; dashboard program_scope=false with scope_preacher_id=owner.
- (decision) An Assistant gains the supervisor's operational surfaces on the patch: /contacts overview/grid/reports, /manage, /sessions, /dashboard, /campaigns/**, /contact, and the /api/manage/contacts/** writes. Staff administration stays out: /admin/**, /volunteers, staff role/status/supervisor changes, caller deactivation.
- (decision) Applies to every Assistant, not only Admin-assigned ones, so the role stays one construct: an Assistant mirrors their supervisor's patch and operational surfaces.
- (decision) Keep assigned_preacher_id as the stored name. Its contract becomes assigned supervisor (Active Preacher or Admin) whose personal patch the Assistant shares. No rename in this slice.
- (decision) Missing, inactive, or non-eligible supervisor yields the existing empty scope with an explanation: never a widening, never a refusal.
- (decision) Every operational surface resolves one patch owner: self for Admin/Preacher, supervisor for Assistant. No surface re-derives 'preacher' or filters directly on staff.userId for scope.
- (decision) The Assistant shares the supervisor's write set on the patch (contact create/update/delete/bulk/photo), because the grid is the operational surface. Staff and account writes remain out.
- (constraint) Existing RLS helpers caller_assigned_preacher_id() and caller_effective_location_ids() are role-agnostic about the assignee and already resolve an Admin id; the app-level resolvers are the lock.
- (constraint) Attendance RLS helper caller_can_read_attendance_session() matches preacher_id only, while the patch predicate includes sessions created_by the owner. RLS and app scope must be aligned or /manage attendance diverges.
- (assumption) Any Admin may bind an Assistant to any Active Admin or Preacher; a Preacher's invite remains self-only; the supervisor must be Active in the same programme.
- (deferred) Renaming assigned_preacher_id to supervisor_user_id, and the related RLS function and parameter names, is deferred: mechanical and behaviour-neutral.
